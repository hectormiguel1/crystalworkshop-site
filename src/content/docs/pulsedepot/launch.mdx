After [Apply](../review-and-apply/), your mods are already on disk. Start the game the way you always do, or from PulseDepot.

<Steps>

1. **Restart the game after every Apply.** Crux reads the Crux image when the game starts.

2. **Start the game from Steam** as usual, **or open Game launcher** in PulseDepot and choose **Launch with Steam**. The launcher runs `steam -applaunch <app id>` with the options shown there: resolution, fullscreen, Japanese voices, shadow resolution, MSAA and swapped confirm/cancel buttons. LR has no display options.

   ![The Game launcher page](../../../assets/shots/pd/launcher.webp)

   On Windows without a known `steam.exe`, PulseDepot opens a `steam://rungameid` link instead, and these options do not apply.

3. **Check that the mods loaded.** Mods with in-game settings, such as Hard Mode, add their pages under **Configuration › Mod Settings** (with XIII Mod Settings installed). If nothing changed in game, see [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/).

</Steps>

## Platform notes

<Tabs syncKey="os">
<TabItem label="Windows">

Nothing extra is needed: Crux is either the NovaFix addon or the standalone `version.dll` beside the game executable, and the game loads it on start.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Linux">

With the **standalone** Crux under Proton, keep this in the game's Steam launch options (**Properties › General › Launch options**):

```
WINEDLLOVERRIDES="version=n,b" %command%
```

Without it, Proton uses Wine's own `version.dll` and Crux never loads. With the NovaFix addon, keep whatever NovaFix itself needs.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Playing offline (Steam Offline Mode)

PulseDepot and Crux need no internet connection: Apply works from the files you added, and Crux reads the Crux image from your game folder when the game starts. Nothing is downloaded at launch.

To play without a connection, put Steam in **Offline Mode** (Steam menu › **Go Offline…**), then start the game from your Steam library or with **Launch with Steam** in PulseDepot. Both go through the Steam client, which starts the game the same way in Offline Mode. Steam's own rules apply: Offline Mode only works after Steam has been online once on this PC with your account remembered.
