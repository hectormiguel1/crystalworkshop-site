The mods released here are **not** open source. For every mod on this site:

- Free to download and use for personal play.
- Please do not re-upload a mod or share the file anywhere else. Share a link to the official mod page instead.
- Do not modify, decompile for re-release, or sell a mod or any part of it without the author's written permission.
