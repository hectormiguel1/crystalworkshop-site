## For developers

Mods can ship native extensions that Crux loads into the game: hooks,
in-game settings pages and state that follows your save slot. The
[Extension SDK](sdk/) guide shows how to write one in Rust with the
open-source [workshop-ext-sdk](https://github.com/hectormiguel1/workshop-extension-sdk)
(MIT or Apache-2.0) and package it with CrystalWorkshop.

:::caution[First public releases]
PulseDepot is in **beta** and CrystalWorkshop in **alpha**: both are tested in game, but expect rough edges. Mods never change your save files, and uninstalling them leaves your saves untouched. If something goes wrong, attach the Crux log when you report a problem.
:::
