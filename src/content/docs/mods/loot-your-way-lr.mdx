Choose what LIGHTNING RETURNS: FINAL FANTASY XIII gives you after battles and from treasure:

- **Drop chances**: keep the game's chances, add a **Drop chance bonus** (0–900%), or set a **Fixed drop chance** (0–100%) for every drop an enemy has.
- **Drop quantities**: a fixed **Drop quantity** for every drop (0 keeps the game's count), or a **Drop quantity multiplier** (10–1000%). Key items always keep their count.
- **Treasure boxes**: **Modify treasure quantities** with an optional **Base item quantity** (0 keeps the game's count) and an **All treasure items** multiplier (10–1000%). Gil and key items stay unchanged.
- **Item stack cap**: how many of one item you can carry, from 99 (the game's limit) up to 999 (under **Advanced**).

Loot Your Way is set for each save slot.

## Requirements

- LIGHTNING RETURNS: FINAL FANTASY XIII, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game and adds the in-game **Mod Settings** menu.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `lr-loot-your-way-0.2.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select LIGHTNING RETURNS: FINAL FANTASY XIII. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game as usual.

</Steps>

## Settings

Open **Configuration → Mod Settings → Loot Your Way**.

- Your settings are stored with your next game save and come back when you load that slot.
- Settings you change in the menu are kept only if you save the game afterwards.

## Compatibility

- Other mods that also change drops or rewards may stack with Loot Your Way or override it.
- If your game version is not recognised, the mod stays inactive and the game plays as usual. The reason is written to the Crux log.

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.

Your saves are never changed by the mod itself, and the items and gil you already received stay yours.
