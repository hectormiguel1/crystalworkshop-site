Pain Your Way makes the enemies in LIGHTNING RETURNS: FINAL FANTASY XIII tougher. The HP, Strength and Magic multipliers apply on top of the difficulty you picked. When a battle starts, each enemy is made stronger:

- its maximum HP, Strength and Magic are multiplied by amounts you choose;
- optionally, its stats are raised to at least a share of your battle party's average.

Pain Your Way is set for each save slot.

## Features

- **Stronger enemies.** Multipliers for enemy max HP, Strength and Magic. 100% is the normal game value. The multipliers apply on top of the difficulty you picked.
- **Minimums based on your party.** Optional minimums for enemy HP, Strength and Magic, as a percentage of your battle party's average (off by default).
- **Outnumber guard.** When the enemies outnumber your party by the ratio you choose, the party-based minimums are skipped for that battle (off by default).
- **Bosses.** Bosses are made stronger too, unless you turn this off.

The Easy-mode override is not available in LIGHTNING RETURNS.

## Requirements

- LIGHTNING RETURNS: FINAL FANTASY XIII, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game and adds the in-game **Mod Settings** menu.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `lr-pain-your-way-0.2.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select LIGHTNING RETURNS: FINAL FANTASY XIII. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game as usual.

</Steps>

## Settings

Open **Configuration → Mod Settings → Pain Your Way**.

- Your settings are stored with your next game save and come back when you load that slot.
- Changes take effect at the next battle or retry.
- Settings you change in the menu are kept only if you save the game afterwards.

## Compatibility

- If your game version is not recognised, the mod stays inactive and the game plays as usual. The reason is written to the Crux log.
- Other mods that also change enemy stats may stack with Pain Your Way or override it.

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.

Your saves are never changed by this mod and load as normal saves.
