Choose what FINAL FANTASY XIII-2 gives you after battles and from treasure:

- **Drop chances**: keep the game's chances, add a bonus to them, or set a fixed chance for common and rare drops;
- **Drop quantities**: how many items a common or rare drop gives;
- **Monster crystals**: the chance that a defeated enemy leaves its crystal;
- **Battle item**: the chance and quantity of a battle's own item;
- **Battle rewards**: Crystarium Points (CP) and gil, each with its own rate (0–1000%);
- **Drop-up abilities**: how strong their effect is;
- **Treasure boxes**: item quantities from treasure;
- **Item stack cap**: how many of one item you can carry (99 to 999).

Loot Your Way is set for each save slot.

## Requirements

- FINAL FANTASY XIII-2, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- The Microsoft Store version is experimental and not tested yet.
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game and adds the in-game **Mod Settings** menu.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `xiii2-loot-your-way-0.2.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select FINAL FANTASY XIII-2. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game as usual.

</Steps>

## Settings

Open **Configuration → Mod Settings → Loot Your Way**.

- Your settings are stored with your next game save and come back when you load that slot.
- Settings you change in the menu are kept only if you save the game afterwards.

## Compatibility

- Other mods that also change drops or rewards may stack with Loot Your Way or override it.
- If your game version is not recognised, the mod stays inactive and the game plays as usual. The reason is written to the Crux log.

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.

Your saves are never changed by the mod itself, and the items and gil you already received stay yours.

## Known issues

- This build for FINAL FANTASY XIII-2 (Steam and Microsoft Store) has not been tested in game yet. Please report problems and attach the Crux log.
