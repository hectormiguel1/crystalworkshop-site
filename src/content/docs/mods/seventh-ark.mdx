Restores the Seventh Ark in FINAL FANTASY XIII: its four trials, Ark Network warps to other places in the game, a fallen stone guardian who briefs you on the trials, and a reward for each trial.

## Features

- **The Ark's trials.** The Ark's trials and their labels are restored. The party must complete the trials before they can face Nemesis and leave the Ark.
- **Ark Network warps.** Warps between the Ark, Orphan's Cradle, Eden, Gran Pulse and the Narthex. The teleporter ring at the Ark's entrance glows like the other Ark Network rings, shows the WARP prompt and plays the warp effect when you leave through it.
- **A fallen guardian.** A fallen stone guardian statue from Taejin's Tower kneels in the entrance hall, by the door to the final chamber. On your first visit, right after the arrival scene, the camera moves to the statue, which speaks four voiced, subtitled lines about the Ark's trials. The briefing plays once; later visits skip it. Skipping it fades the picture back in and gives you control.
- **Trial progress.** Examine the statue to see how many of the Ark's four trials you have completed.
- **Rewards.** Each trial's reward chest in the entrance hall holds a unique accessory: Genji Glove (Extermination), Growth Egg (Target), Tetradic Tiara (Warp) and Connoisseur Catalog (Treasure). The Treasure Trial's spheres each hold 5,000 gil. Nemesis awards 75,000 CP and can drop Dark Matter (common) or a Trapezohedron (rare).
- **Datalog.** Restored Datalog entries.

## Requirements

- FINAL FANTASY XIII, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `seventh-ark-0.1.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select FINAL FANTASY XIII. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game from Steam as usual.

</Steps>

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.
