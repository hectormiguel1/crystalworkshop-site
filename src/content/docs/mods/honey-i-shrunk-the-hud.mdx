Shrinks or enlarges the battle and field HUD of FINAL FANTASY XIII, around the centre of the screen. Menus always stay at their original size.

## Features

- **HUD size.** 25 to 200%, 100% by default. 100% is the original size.
- **Menus unchanged.** Menus, the pause screen, shops and the Crystarium always stay at their original size.
- **Strike a Pose.** Works with Strike a Pose.

## Requirements

- FINAL FANTASY XIII, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game and adds the in-game **Mod Settings** menu.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `xiii-honey-i-shrunk-the-hud-0.1.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select FINAL FANTASY XIII. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game from Steam as usual.

</Steps>

## Settings

Open **Configuration → Mod Settings → Honey, I Shrunk the HUD** and set **HUD size**. Your saved HUD size is kept.

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.
