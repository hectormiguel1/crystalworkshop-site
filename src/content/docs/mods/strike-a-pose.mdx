Strike a Pose is an experimental photo mode for FINAL FANTASY XIII. Pause the scene, fly the camera freely, pose the characters already in the scene, and hide the panel for a clean screenshot.

## Features

- **Opens from the main menu.** On a field, open the main menu and select **Photo mode (experimental)** after the game's own entries. The menu closes, the scene pauses, and a compact panel headed **PHOTO MODE** opens at the top right. Choosing **Exit photo mode** puts characters back, removes the panel, resumes the scene and returns control to you.
- **World paused, audio on.** While photo mode is open the world stays paused but audio keeps playing, and the field minimap is hidden. It comes back when you choose **Exit photo mode**; your map setting is not changed. Nearby interaction prompts, such as a Cie'th stone, are not triggered by the panel buttons.
- **Camera.**
  - **Control camera** flies freely with the controller: left stick moves, right stick looks around, LB/L1 lowers and RB/R1 raises the camera. Back or Confirm returns to the panel.
  - **Move speed** sets the flight speed.
  - Lens and roll adjustments.
  - **Store camera** and **Recall camera** keep one camera position and lens while the panel is open; **Reset camera** returns to the starting camera.
  - **Clear view** hides the panel so you can take a clean screenshot with your usual screenshot tool. Back or Confirm brings the panel back.
- **Characters.**
  - Pick a character in the scene, change its position and orientation, and confirm **Apply placement**. **Restore character** puts it back.
  - **Animation** lists the animations already loaded for the selected character. **Animation position** poses it anywhere from 0 to 100% through that animation; its weapon follows.
  - **Restore pose** and **Original animation** bring back the starting pose. **Exit photo mode** restores it too.
- **Honey, I Shrunk the HUD.** With Honey, I Shrunk the HUD installed, the panel keeps your chosen HUD size and stays at the top right.

The panel uses the game's own selectors and dropdowns. Confirm the section selector to choose **Camera**, **Characters** or **Scene**; Left/Right also switches sections and adjusts values.

## Requirements

- FINAL FANTASY XIII, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `strike-a-pose-0.1.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select FINAL FANTASY XIII. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game from Steam as usual.

</Steps>

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, select Strike a Pose, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.

## Known issues

- **Photo mode (experimental)** is only in the main menu, not the Start-button pause screen, and has no hotkey. It refuses to open outside a stable field scene or on an unsupported game version.
- Only characters already in the scene can be moved; absent party members cannot be added. The character list uses the scene's own names rather than a party roster.
- Some characters' animations cannot be posed; the panel says when one is unavailable.
- Named pose presets, facial expressions, lighting, and separate freeze or HUD toggles are not available.
- Not yet verified in game on every supported game version.
