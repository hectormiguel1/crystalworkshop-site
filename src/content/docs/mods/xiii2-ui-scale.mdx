Resize the field and battle HUD of FINAL FANTASY XIII-2. You set the size separately for the field and for battle, from 25% to 200%. The default for both is **50%**; 100% is the normal size.

## Features

- **Field HUD:**
  - the Moogle Clock, kept at the bottom centre;
  - conversation and message windows, and the automatic conversation log;
  - cinema subtitles and area titles.
- **Battle HUD:**
  - command, ability, target, item and paradigm lists;
  - the round command/ATB ornament;
  - party HP and ATB;
  - target information and the stagger gauge;
  - drive gauges and buttons;
  - damage numbers.
- **Feral Link prompt.** The prompt stays above the HP panel. Its gauge keeps its normal size and position.
- **Cinematic actions.** The button prompts and their captions are scaled in place. Button timing is unchanged.
- **Cursor, target markers and clipping** all follow the same scale.
- **Normal size kept for:**
  - menus;
  - the Historia Crux;
  - battle scenes and models;
  - the minimap.

## Requirements

- FINAL FANTASY XIII-2, Steam version, on Windows or on Linux (Proton).
- The Microsoft Store version is experimental and not tested yet.
- **PulseDepot** (beta), the mod manager. It also installs **Crux**, which loads this mod in the game.
- No separate settings mod is needed: this mod adds its own **Mod Settings** page.

## Install

<Steps>

1. Download `xiii2-ui-scale-0.1.0.cws`.
2. In PulseDepot, select FINAL FANTASY XIII-2. Choose **Add mod…** and add the file.
3. Choose **Apply changes**.
4. Start the game as usual.

</Steps>

## Settings

Open **Configuration → Mod Settings** and use Left/Right:

| Setting | Range | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field HUD Scale | 25–200% in steps of 5 | 50% | Size of the field HUD |
| Battle HUD Scale | 25–200% in steps of 5 | 50% | Size of the battle HUD |

- Changes apply immediately and are kept for your next launch.
- On this page, the game's **Restore Defaults** sets both values back to 50% and leaves the game's own options alone.

## Compatibility

- Steam version: tested in game.
- Microsoft Store version: experimental, not tested yet.
- The two values are the same for every save slot and are kept by Crux. Your saves and the game's own options are never changed.
- If another mod already changes the HUD in the same places, this mod leaves them alone and stays inactive. The reason is written to the Crux log.
- Works alongside Void Beyond — Historia Crux Entry.

## Uninstall

In PulseDepot, choose **Remove from library**, then **Apply changes**.

The HUD returns to its normal size. Your saves are never changed by this mod.

## Known issues

- Microsoft Store version not tested. If you try it, please report the result and attach the Crux log.
- HUD parts the mod does not know keep their normal size.
