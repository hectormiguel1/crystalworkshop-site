PulseDepot never removes Crux, even when you uninstall every mod. Remove it by hand:

<Steps>

1. In PulseDepot, choose **Uninstall all…**, then **Apply changes**. See [Uninstall](../../pulsedepot/uninstall/).

2. Delete the Crux build you have:

   - the addon: `Crux.dll` in the NovaFix `addons` folder;
   - the standalone build: `version.dll` beside the game executable.

3. On Linux, remove `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="version=n,b" %command%` from the game's Steam launch options.

4. Optional: delete `Crux.ini`, the `Crux.jsonl` logs (in the `addons` folder) and `mod-state.dat` (the mods' settings, in the mod folder beside `active.crximg`).

</Steps>

Your saves are untouched: Crux never reads or writes them.
