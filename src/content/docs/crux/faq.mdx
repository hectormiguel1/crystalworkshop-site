## Do I download Crux separately?

No. It ships inside the PulseDepot package, and PulseDepot installs it when you apply.

## Is Crux a replacement for NovaFix?

No. NovaFix is a separate third-party fix for the games. Crux runs as a NovaFix addon when NovaFix is installed, and on its own (as `version.dll`) when it is not.

## Why does Linux need a launch option?

Without NovaFix, Crux is the game's `version.dll`. Proton prefers Wine's built-in `version.dll` unless `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="version=n,b" %command%` is set in the game's launch options.

## Where are my mods' settings?

In `mod-state.dat` in the Crux mod folder, kept per save slot. Your save files are never read or written.

## How do I know Crux is running?

`Crux.jsonl` appears in the `addons` folder beside the game executable after the game starts. See [Logs](../logs/).

## Is Crux open source?

No. Crux, like PulseDepot and CrystalWorkshop, is freeware, all rights reserved. See the [licence](../../legal/).
