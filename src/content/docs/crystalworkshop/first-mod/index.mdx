In this track you make a small, complete mod for FINAL FANTASY XIII: **a cheaper Potion in the shops**. It touches every part of the workflow once, so the bigger guides make sense afterwards.

**You need:** [CrystalWorkshop installed](../installing/), a Steam install of FINAL FANTASY XIII, and [PulseDepot](../../pulsedepot/install/) for step 5. About 15 minutes.

## What happens to your game

Nothing in the game archives changes, at any step. CrystalWorkshop keeps your pending changes in its own work folder (`ff13_studio/` in the game folder) until you export. The shard you export stores your edit as a patch against each player's own game files, and PulseDepot installs it through Crux. Your saves are never written.

When you are done, continue with the editing guides in the sidebar: they go from simple data and text edits to models, effects, scenes and scripts.
